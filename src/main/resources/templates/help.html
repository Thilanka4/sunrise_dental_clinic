Staff Guide

A quick reference for the four things you'll do most often. For anything not covered here, ask a colleague or your system administrator.

1. Signing in

  1. Go to the login page and enter the staff username and password you were given.
  2. Click Sign in — you'll land on the dashboard.
  3. Use Sign out (top right of every page) when you're done.

2. Registering a new appointment

  1. Click Register in the nav bar (or "Register New Appointment" on the dashboard).
  2. Fill in the patient's name, address, contact number, the dentist, the treatment, and the date/time.
  3. Click Register appointment. You'll be shown the new appointment number (e.g. APT000001) — give this to the patient.
  4. If the dentist already has an appointment at that time, you'll see an error — pick another time.

3. Searching for an appointment

  1. Click Find in the nav bar.
  2. Enter the appointment number and click Search.
  3. You'll see the full appointment details, including its current status.

4. Generating a bill

  1. Open the appointment (via Find, or from the dashboard's Today's Appointments list).
  2. Click Generate / View Bill. This also marks the appointment as completed.
  3. From the bill page you can Print it or Download PDF.
  4. A bill can only be generated once per appointment — opening it again just shows the same bill.
